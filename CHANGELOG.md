## [0.9.0] - 2026-11-27

The first public release.

### The game

- Enemies march down a bridge toward your squad; type the word on one to shoot it.
- Lock on with the first letter, Backspace to switch words, and a short rifle jam for a
  wrong letter.
- Streak multipliers, waves, and airstrikes you earn back by finishing words.
- Three enemy types: Mines, Destroyers with homing missiles, and Oppressors with fans of
  bullets, whose missiles and bullets carry words too.
- Run history with charts of score, accuracy, speed and streak, and new-record callouts.
- How to play, from the title, pause and game-over screens.
- Options: music and sound volume, larger word labels, high-contrast colours, reduced
  motion, screen shake, gameplay logs, update check, and resetting run history.

### Where it runs

- A web version on GitHub Pages: nothing to install.
- An offline version for Windows, macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel) and Linux (AppImage,
  `.deb`, `.rpm`), which remembers its window size and can go fullscreen with F11.
- The offline version tells you when a new version is out and installs it for you.
